Invitation orders

Your simplified workflow — client orders online, you design in Canva, you send the finished file directly.

Your workflow
01

Receive the order

Client submits their details on the website. You get an email and WhatsApp notification with everything they've chosen.

02

Request their assets

Send them the personalisation instructions below — they email you their photos and any extra details.

03

Design & deliver

Design in Canva using their chosen style. Email the finished JPG or PDF directly to the client. Done.

Personalisation request — copy & send to client

What to send your client after receiving their order

Copy the template below and send it to the client from your email. Fill in their order number and style before sending.

Hi [Client Name], Thank you so much for your order — I'm so excited to create your invitation! To get started, could you please send the following to hello@momentsbyshanaz.co.uk with the subject line: 📧 Subject: Order #[ORDER NUMBER] — [STYLE NAME] Invitation Please include: • Any photos you'd like included (high resolution, JPG or PNG) • Exact names, dates and venue details as you'd like them to appear • Any colours, fonts or specific styling requests • Any wording you'd like to include (RSVP details, dress code etc.) Once I receive everything I'll get started and have your invitation ready within 24 hours. If you have any questions, just reply to this email! With love, Shanaz Moments by Shanaz momentsbyshanaz.co.uk
✓ Copied!
Tip: Replace [Client Name], [ORDER NUMBER] and [STYLE NAME] before sending. The order number comes from the Etsy or email confirmation. The style name is what they selected on the order form.

✦  File name: FirstName_Invitation_MBS.jpg — keeps things organised in your inbox
